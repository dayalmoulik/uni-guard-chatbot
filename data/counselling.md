## Counselling and Seat Allocation

The **counselling process** for C-DAC’s Post Graduate Certificate Programmes is an **online procedure** where qualified candidates fill in their **preferences for courses and training centres**. The admission process involves **two counselling sessions** and **three rounds of seat allocation**.

### **First Counselling Session**
*   **Process:** Candidates must log in to the C-DAC website to enter their order of preferences for courses and training centres. This session is scheduled from **January 22 to January 28, 2026**.
*   **Eligibility and Choice Filling:** Candidates can only select courses for which they meet the eligibility criteria based on their C-CAT ranks and application details. Candidates are encouraged to give as many preferences as desired to increase their chances of allocation.
*   **Saving Preferences:** Preferences must be saved before logging out; unsaved lists will be lost. Candidates can modify their saved list until the closing date of the counselling session.
*   **Strict Adherence:** A candidate will **not be considered** for any course or training centre that was not included in their preference list.

### **Seat Allocation (Rounds 1 and 2)**
*   **First Round (January 30, 2026):** Seats are allocated strictly based on **C-CAT ranks and the order of preferences** submitted.
*   **Confirmation of Seat:** To confirm a seat allocated in the first round, candidates must pay the **first installment fee (Rs 10,000 + GST)** by February 4, 2026. 
*   **Locking/Freezing:** After payment, candidates may "lock/freeze" their seat. If they do not lock it, they will automatically be considered for their **higher-preferred choices** in the second round, provided they have paid the first installment.
*   **Second Round (February 6, 2026):** Candidates who were not offered their top preferences in the first round (and paid the first installment) or those who were not allocated a seat at all will be considered based on their **original preferences**. Seats allocated in this round are **final**.

### **Second Counselling Session (Round 3)**
*   **Purpose:** This session is held to fill **vacant seats** remaining after the second round. It is scheduled from **February 11 to February 16, 2026**.
*   **Eligibility:** This session is open to candidates who did not participate in the first counselling, were not allocated a seat previously, or failed to confirm/withdrew their previous admission.
*   **Caution Deposit:** To participate in the second counselling, candidates must pay a **caution deposit of Rs 10,000 + GST**.
*   **Third Round Allocation (February 17, 2026):** This is based on **new preferences** submitted during the second counselling. 
*   **Refund Rules:** The caution deposit is **non-refundable** if a seat is allocated in the third round, regardless of whether the candidate joins. If no seat is allocated, the full caution deposit is refunded.

### **Important Constraints**
*   **No Manual Transfers:** Once a seat is allocated at a specific centre, manual requests for transfers are generally not considered.
*   **Preference Priority:** A candidate **cannot opt for a lower-preferred choice** if they have been allocated a higher-preference seat in any round.
