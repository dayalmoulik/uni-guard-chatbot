## Hardware & Software Requirements

For the February 2026 batch of C-DAC Post Graduate Certificate Programmes, hardware and software requirements are primarily defined for students enrolled in **online mode** courses to ensure they can effectively participate from their homes. It is explicitly stated that joining online classes from **mobile phones is not advisable**.

### **General Hardware Requirements**
Across all online courses, students are required to have a laptop or desktop with the following general specifications:
*   **Internet:** A high-speed, uninterrupted connection with at least **25 Mbps speed** (Upload/Download).
*   **Accessories:** A functional **web camera** and a **headphone with a microphone** are mandatory.
*   **Storage:** A minimum hard disk capacity of **500 GB** is required for all programs.

### **Course-Specific Hardware Configurations**
The required processor, memory, and graphics capabilities vary depending on the technical demands of the specific programme:

| Programme | Processor (Minimum) | RAM (Minimum) | Video/Graphics Card |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PGCP-AC** | Intel Core i3 or above | 8 GB | Intel Integrated Graphics |
| **PGCP-BDA** | Intel Core i5 or above | 8 GB | Intel Integrated (4 GB NVIDIA suggested) |
| **PGCP-AI** | Intel Core i5 or above | 8 GB | **4 GB NVIDIA** (GeForce GTX10 series+) |
| **PGCP-FBD** | Intel Core i5 or above | 8 GB | **4 GB NVIDIA** (GeForce GTX10 series+) |
| **PGCP-CSF** | Intel Core i5 or above | 8 GB (**16 GB** recommended) | Intel Integrated Graphics |

**Special Note for PGCP-CSF:** For this programme, **virtualization (VT-x/Vx) must be enabled in the BIOS** of the client machine.

### **Software Requirements**
Students must prepare their systems with appropriate operating systems and development tools before classes begin:
*   **Operating Systems:** Most courses require **Windows 10** or a **64-bit Linux** distribution such as Ubuntu, Fedora, or CentOS.
*   **Common Applications:** All systems should have the latest versions of Mozilla Firefox or Google Chrome, and an office suite like Microsoft Office or Open Office.
*   **Development Environments:** Specific tools are required based on the course modules:
    *   **PGCP-AC:** Requires GCC, MS Visual Studio, JDK 11+, Eclipse, and MySQL.
    *   **PGCP-BDA:** Requires Virtual Box, Python 3.3.4, R Studio, Hadoop, Spark, and Tableau.
    *   **PGCP-AI:** Requires Jupyter notebook, Python 3.6 (with NumPy/SciPy), TensorFlow, PyTorch, and OpenCV.
    *   **PGCP-CSF:** Requires specialized security tools like Nmap, Wireshark, Metasploit, Burp Suite, and various forensics tools like Autopsy and WinHex.
    *   **PGCP-FBD:** Requires JDK, Android SDK, Node.js, Docker, and Blockchain platforms like Truffle or Hardhat.
