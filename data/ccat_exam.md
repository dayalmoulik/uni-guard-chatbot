## Common Admission Test (C-CAT)

C-DAC’s **Common Admission Test (C-CAT)** is the mandatory entrance examination for students seeking admission into any of the 12 Post Graduate Certificate Programmes for the February 2026 batch,. 

### **Exam Structure and Syllabus**
The C-CAT is divided into three sections, each lasting one hour. The specific sections a candidate must take depend on the category of the course they select,:

*   **Section A:** Covers English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, Computer Fundamentals, and Concepts of Programming.
*   **Section B:** Includes C Programming, Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming Concepts using C++, Operating Systems & Networking, and Basics of Big Data & Artificial Intelligence.
*   **Section C:** Focuses on Computer Architecture, Digital Electronics, and Microprocessors.

Candidates applying for **Category II** courses must appear for Sections A and B, while those applying for **Category III** courses (such as VLSI Design or Embedded Systems Design) must appear for all three sections (A, B, and C),,.

### **Scoring and Marking Scheme**
The exam consists of **50 objective-type questions per section**, with a maximum score of **150 marks per section**. The marking scheme is as follows:
*   **+3 marks** for every correct answer.
*   **-1 mark** for every wrong answer.
*   **0 marks** for unattempted questions.

### **Ranking and Qualification**
Ranks are awarded based on aggregate performance in the required sections (A+B or A+B+C). However, certain performance thresholds must be met to qualify for a rank:
*   **Section A Threshold:** Candidates in the **lowest 10%** of Section A performance, or those with zero or negative marks in Section A, are disqualified from ranking in any category,.
*   **Section B & C Rules:** Candidates with zero or negative marks in Section B cannot be ranked in Category II or III, and those with zero or negative marks in Section C cannot be ranked in Category III,.
*   **Tie-Breaker:** If scores are tied, the candidate with **higher marks in Section A** is ranked higher. If Section A marks are also the same, the candidate with the higher **accuracy ratio** (correct answers vs. attempted questions) is prioritized.

### **Registration, Fees, and Schedule**
*   **Dates:** For the February 2026 batch, the C-CAT will be held on **January 10 and 11, 2026**,.
*   **Mode:** It is a computer-based test conducted **physically at designated test centres** across various Indian cities,.
*   **Fees:** The fee (inclusive of GST) is **Rs 1,550** for Category II and **Rs 1,750** for Category III. Fees are non-refundable and must be paid online.
*   **Admit Cards:** Candidates must download their admit cards from the C-DAC website between **January 6 and 10, 2026**, and present them with a photo ID at the test center,.

Admissions are offered strictly in the order of these C-CAT ranks and the preferences of courses and centres submitted by the candidates during the online counselling process,.
