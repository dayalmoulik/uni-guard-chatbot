## Code of Conduct & Policies

C-DAC maintains strict **code of conduct and policy guidelines** that apply to candidates throughout the entire admission process, course delivery, and campus placements. By applying for or enrolling in a course, students indicate their full acceptance of these terms.

The key policies and rules of conduct outlined in the Admission Booklet include:

### **Discipline and Decorum**
*   **General Behavior:** Students are expected to maintain discipline and decorum at all times. The use of **unfair means, coercion, violence, indecent language**, or offensive gestures is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate suspension or expulsion.
*   **Official Communication:** Candidates must only use the designated email addresses and phone numbers provided in the sources for queries. Contacting C-DAC officials, training centers, or government bodies directly via private messages or calls is **prohibited**.
*   **Accuracy of Information:** All information provided during application must be correct. Providing false information or failing to meet eligibility requirements at any stage—even during the course—will result in **immediate cancellation of admission**.

### **Academic and Attendance Policies**
*   **Attendance Requirement:** Students must maintain a minimum of **85% overall attendance** to be eligible for the Centralised Mid-Course and Course-End Examinations.
*   **Course Exclusivity:** These are full-time programs. Students are **not permitted** to engage in any other job, profession, or educational course (full-time or part-time) during the course period without prior written permission.
*   **Examination Conduct:** During C-CAT or course exams, students must not use external communication, study materials, or electronic gadgets like calculators and mobile phones. Such actions are treated as "use of unfair means" and may lead to the cancellation of the student's certificate or admission.

### **Administrative Policies and Rights**
*   **Right to Modify:** C-DAC reserves the right to modify or cancel any part of the admission process, including exam dates, syllabus, seat capacities, or course offerings, at any stage without assigning a reason.
*   **Course Viability:** C-DAC may cancel a course at a specific center if it is not viable to conduct it. In such cases, students may be offered a refund or an alternative seat at another center based on their rank.
*   **Verification and Identification:** Candidates must provide additional information for proctoring or security, such as **fingerprints or audio/video surveillance**, during examinations.

### **Cancellation and Refund Policies**
*   **Penalties:** Withdrawing from a course attracts financial penalties that increase as the course commencement date approaches. After March 24, 2026, no refunds are provided.
*   **Ineligibility Penalty:** If a candidate is found ineligible during the registration document verification, the **first installment fee (Rs 10,000 + GST) is non-refundable**.
