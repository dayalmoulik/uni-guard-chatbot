## Course Fee Structure

The fee structure for C-DAC’s Post Graduate Certificate Programmes is determined by the specific **course** and its **mode of delivery** (Online or Physical). All mentioned fees are exclusive of **Goods & Services Tax (GST)**, which is applicable at the prevailing government rate (18% as of November 2025) at the time of payment.

### **Total Course Fees**
The total fee covers class delivery, examinations, final certificates, and placement assistance. The fees for the February 2026 batch are as follows:

*   **Online Courses:**
    *   **PGCP-AC and PGCP-CSF:** Rs 76,500 + GST.
    *   **PGCP-BDA:** Rs 97,750 + GST.
    *   **PGCP-FBD:** Rs 1,06,250 + GST.
    *   **PGCP-AI:** Rs 127,500 + GST.
*   **Physical Courses:**
    *   **PGCP-AC, PGCP-ESD, PGCP-ITISS, PGCP-VLSI, PGCP-MC, PGCP-ASSD, PGCP-HPCSA, and PGCP-RAT:** Rs 90,000 + GST.
    *   **PGCP-BDA:** Rs 1,15,000 + GST.
    *   **PGCP-AI:** Rs 1,50,000 + GST.

### **Payment Installments**
The total fee must be paid in **two installments** exclusively through **online modes** (netbanking, UPI, or credit/debit cards); cash, cheques, or demand drafts will not be accepted.

1.  **First Installment:** A token amount of **Rs 10,000 + GST** is required to provisionally confirm a seat after allocation. 
2.  **Second Installment:** The remaining balance plus GST must be paid by **February 23, 2026**. The specific balance amounts for each course are detailed in the Admission Booklet (e.g., Rs 80,000 + GST for most physical courses).

### **C-CAT and Counselling Fees**
In addition to course fees, there are non-refundable application and participation fees:
*   **C-CAT Fee:** Rs 1,550 for Category II (Sections A+B) and Rs 1,750 for Category III (Sections A+B+C).
*   **Caution Deposit:** Candidates participating in the **second counselling** must pay a **Rs 10,000 + GST** caution deposit. This is non-refundable if a seat is allocated in the third round, but is fully refunded if no seat is allocated.

### **Cancellation and Penalty Rules**
If a candidate withdraws from the programme, penalties are deducted from the refunded balance based on the timing of the request:
*   **Before Course Commencement:** Penalties range from **Rs 1,000 to Rs 10,000 + GST** depending on the specific date of withdrawal.
*   **After Commencement (Feb 25 – March 24, 2026):** **50% of the total course fee** plus GST is forfeited.
*   **After March 24, 2026:** No refund is provided.
*   **Ineligibility:** If a candidate is found ineligible during document verification, the **first installment (Rs 10,000 + GST) will not be refunded**.

### **Hostel and Canteen Fees**
For fixed prices for residential facilities, Candidates must contact the **Administrative Officers** at their respective training centres to obtain specific information regarding hostel and canteen costs.
