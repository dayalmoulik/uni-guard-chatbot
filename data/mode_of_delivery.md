## Mode of Delivery

The C-DAC Post Graduate Certificate Programmes for the February 2026 batch are 24-week, full-time courses consisting of **1,200 total hours** (900 hours of theory, lab, and project work plus 300 hours of self-study). The delivery of these programs is categorized into two primary modes: **Fully Online** and **Fully Physical**.

### **Classification by Mode**
The mode of delivery is determined by the specific course and the training center offering it:
*   **Flexible (Online or Physical):** The PG Certificate Programmes in Advanced Computing (**PGCP-AC**), Big Data Analytics (**PGCP-BDA**), and Artificial Intelligence (**PGCP-AI**) are delivered in either fully online or fully physical modes depending on the center.
*   **Strictly Physical:** The programs for Embedded Systems Design (**PGCP-ESD**), IT Infrastructure, Systems & Security (**PGCP-ITISS**), Mobile Computing (**PGCP-MC**), VLSI Design (**PGCP-VLSI**), Advanced Secure Software Development (**PGCP-ASSD**), HPC System Administration (**PGCP-HPCSA**), and Robotics & Allied Technologies (**PGCP-RAT**) are delivered **only in physical mode** at all training centers.
*   **Strictly Online:** The programs for FinTech & Blockchain Development (**PGCP-FBD**) and Cyber Security & Forensics (**PGCP-CSF**) are delivered **exclusively in online mode**.

### **Online Mode Requirements**
Students enrolled in online courses participate from their homes or personal spaces. Key requirements include:
*   **Technical Infrastructure:** Students must have their own laptop or PC with a webcam, microphone, and a high-speed, uninterrupted internet connection with at least **25 Mbps speed**. 
*   **Devices:** C-DAC explicitly advises against joining online classes from mobile phones.
*   **Hardware Specifications:** Minimum hardware often includes an Intel Core i3 or i5 processor and 8 GB of RAM, though specific courses like PGCP-AI may require a 4 GB NVIDIA graphics card.

### **Physical Mode Specifics**
Physical courses require students to attend sessions at the assigned training center.
*   **Schedule:** Most centers conduct theory and lab sessions for **6 to 8 hours per day**, six days a week.
*   **Facilities:** Students in physical mode may have access to hostel and canteen facilities at select centers on a first-come, first-served basis.

### **Mandatory Physical Presence for Examinations**
Regardless of the course delivery mode (online or physical), all **Centralised Mid-Course Examinations (CMCE)** and **Centralised Course-End Examinations (CCEE)** are conducted **physically**. 
*   **Location:** Students in physical mode take exams at their training center, while online students must travel to an exam city of their choice from a provided list.
*   **Attendance:** An overall **attendance of 85% is mandatory** for any student to be eligible to appear for these examinations.
