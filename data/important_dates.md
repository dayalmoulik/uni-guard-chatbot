The admission process and course timeline for the **February 2026 batch** of C-DAC Post Graduate Certificate Programmes are defined by several critical milestones, ranging from registration to the final examination.

### **Registration and Entrance Exam (C-CAT)**
*   **November 27, 2025:** Online registration and applications for C-CAT begin.
*   **December 11, 2025:** A Pan-CDAC online Q&A session regarding the application process is held at 3:00 pm.
*   **December 29, 2025:** **Closing of online registration**, application, and examination fee payment for C-CAT.
*   **January 6 – 10, 2026:** Candidates can download their C-CAT admit cards.
*   **January 10 – 11, 2026:** C-DAC’s **Common Admission Test (C-CAT)** is conducted at test centres in computerized mode.

### **Counselling and Seat Allocation**
*   **January 22, 2026:** Announcement of C-CAT ranks.
*   **January 22 – 28, 2026:** **First Counselling Session**, where candidates select their order of preferences for courses and centres online.
*   **January 30, 2026:** Declaration of the **first round of seat allocation**.
*   **February 4, 2026:** Deadline for paying the first installment fee (Rs 10,000 + GST) for those allocated seats in the first round.
*   **February 6, 2026:** Declaration of the **second round of seat allocation**.
*   **February 10, 2026:** Deadline for paying the first installment fee for those allocated seats for the first time in the second round.
*   **February 11 – 16, 2026:** **Second Counselling Session**, involving the payment of a caution deposit and fresh selection of courses/centres for vacant seats.
*   **February 17, 2026:** Declaration of the **third round of seat allocation**.

### **Final Admission and Course Timeline**
*   **February 23, 2026:** Final deadline for the **payment of the balance course fee**.
*   **February 24, 2026:** Last date for registration of students and uploading of eligibility certificates.
*   **February 25, 2026:** **Commencement of all PG Certificate Programmes** across India in online or physical mode.
*   **May 2026:** Centralised Mid-Course Examinations (CMCE) for the batch.
*   **July 2026:** Centralised Course-End Examinations (CCEE) for the batch.
*   **August 11, 2026:** Formal completion of the PG Certificate Programmes.

### **Important Note for Result-Awaiting Candidates**
Candidates appearing for their final degree exams in 2026 must complete all parts of their qualifying exam by the course start date (February 25, 2026) and submit proof of passing with the required marks by **June 30, 2026**.
