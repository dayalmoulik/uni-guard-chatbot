## C-CAT Scoring and Ranking

The scoring and ranking for C-DAC’s Common Admission Test (C-CAT) are governed by specific rules regarding marking, qualification thresholds, and tie-breaking procedures.

### **C-CAT Scoring System**
*   **Marking Scheme:** Every section in the C-CAT contains 50 objective-type questions. Candidates earn **+3 marks for each correct answer**, while **-1 mark is deducted for each wrong answer**. Un-attempted questions receive 0 marks.
*   **Maximum Marks:** Since each section has 50 questions, the maximum marks a candidate can obtain in any single section is **150**.
*   **Section Combinations:** Candidates are scored based on the sections required for their chosen course category (Sections A+B for Category II or Sections A+B+C for Category III).

### **Ranking Criteria**
*   **Rank Types:** Qualified candidates are provided ranks based on their aggregate performance. **Category II ranks** are derived from the total marks in Sections A+B, while **Category III ranks** are derived from Sections A+B+C.
*   **Multiple Ranks:** Candidates who apply for Category III will receive both a Category II and a Category III rank, provided they qualify in the respective sections.
*   **Validity:** These rank-lists are applicable only for admission to the **February 2026 batch**.

### **Qualification Thresholds**
Not all candidates who appear for the exam will receive a rank. The Admission Booklet outline several disqualification criteria:
*   **Section A Requirement:** Candidates who fall into the **lowest 10% of performers in Section A** are disqualified from ranking.
*   **Zero/Negative Marks:** Even if a candidate is above the lowest 10%, they will not be ranked if they have **zero or negative total marks in Section A**.
*   **Category-Specific Disqualification:** Candidates with zero or negative marks in **Section B** will not receive a rank in either Category II or III. Similarly, those with zero or negative marks in **Section C** will not receive a rank in Category III.
*   **Section A Priority:** If a candidate fails to qualify in Section A due to poor performance, they will not receive a rank in any category, regardless of how well they performed in Sections B or C.

### **Tie-Breaker Rules**
In cases where two or more candidates have the same total marks in the relevant sections, the following rules determine the higher rank:
1.  **Section A Marks:** The candidate with **higher marks specifically in Section A** is awarded the higher rank.
2.  **Accuracy Ratio:** If the tie persists, the candidate with the **higher ratio of "correct answers to attempted questions"** in the sections required for that category is given the higher rank.
3.  **Identical Rank:** If candidates remain tied after both checks, they are assigned the same C-CAT rank.
