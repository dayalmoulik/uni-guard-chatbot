## Refund Policy

The refund policy for C-DAC’s Post Graduate Certificate Programmes (February 2026 batch) is governed by specific rules regarding the timing of withdrawal, candidate eligibility, and the reason for the refund. Candidates are expected to read and understand these rules before paying any fees, as no disputes are entertained once payments are made.

### **C-CAT and Caution Deposit Refunds**
*   **C-CAT Fee:** The application fee for the entrance exam is **non-refundable**.
*   **Caution Deposit:** Candidates participating in the second counselling must pay a caution deposit of **Rs 10,000 + GST**.
    *   If the candidate is **not allocated any seat** in the third round, the full deposit is refunded.
    *   If the candidate **is allocated a seat** in the third round, the caution deposit is **not refunded**, regardless of whether they join the course.

### **Course Fee Withdrawal Penalties**
If a candidate chooses to withdraw from the programme, the amount refunded depends on when the request is submitted. Penalties are deducted from the fees paid, and the balance is typically refunded within **two months** of the course starting.

| Withdrawal Period | Penalty Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Jan 30 – Feb 4, 2026** (Before 5 pm) | Rs 1,000 + GST |
| **Feb 5 – Feb 10, 2026** (Before 5 pm) | Rs 5,000 + GST |
| **Feb 11 – Feb 24, 2026** (Before 5 pm) | Rs 10,000 + GST |
| **Feb 25 – March 24, 2026** (After start) | **50% of the total course fee** + GST |
| **After March 24, 2026** | **No refund** (Full fees forfeited) |

### **Conditions for Non-Refundable Fees**
*   **Ineligibility:** If a candidate is found ineligible during the document verification process at registration, the **first installment (Rs 10,000 + GST) will not be refunded**.
*   **Documentation Failure:** Provisionally admitted students who fail to submit proof of passing their qualifying degree by **June 30, 2026**, are not eligible for any refund.
*   **Transfer Requests:** If a student is granted a transfer to a course with a lower fee, C-DAC will **not refund the difference** in amount.

### **Refunds Initiated by C-DAC**
If C-DAC cancels a course at a specific training centre for any reason (such as viability), the **full course fee paid will be refunded**. In such cases, candidates may also be offered an alternative seat at another centre based on their rank.

### **Withdrawal Procedure**
To initiate a refund before the course begins, candidates must:
1.  Complete the **online withdrawal form** through their C-DAC website login.
2.  Print, sign, and scan the form.
3.  Email the signed form to **actsfinance@cdac.in** with the subject "Request for withdrawal from PG Certificate Programme admission". 
4.  **Note:** Online entries without the accompanying signed email will not be considered for a refund. For withdrawals after the course starts, students must submit the prescribed forms directly at their admitted centre.
