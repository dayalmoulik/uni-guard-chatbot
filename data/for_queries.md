## Contact Details For Queries

For any correspondence regarding the C-DAC Post Graduate Certificate Programmes, candidates must adhere to specific communication protocols. All emails must be sent from the candidate’s **registered email address** and must include their **name and C-CAT Form Number**; otherwise, C-DAC may be unable to respond. Additionally, candidates are prohibited from contacting officials or government bodies directly through private calls or messages.

Following categorized email addresses can be used for specific types of queries:

### **General and Admission-Related Queries**
*   **Registration, Eligibility, and C-CAT:** For queries regarding online registration, C-CAT applications, checking eligibility for specific engineering degrees, city change requests, or issues with downloading admit cards, contact **actssupport@cdac.in**.
*   **Counselling and Seat Allocation:** For issues encountered during the online selection of courses and training centres, or queries regarding seat allocation and course fee payments, contact **course-enquiry@cdac.in**.
*   **Withdrawals and Refunds:** To submit signed withdrawal forms or inquire about the status of a refund, contact **actsfinance@cdac.in**.

### **Centre-Specific Queries**
For information regarding **hostel facilities, canteen costs, or local administrative details**, candidates should contact the Administrative Officers at their respective training centres. Some specific centre emails include:
*   **Bengaluru:** actsb@cdac.in
*   **Chennai:** chnacts@cdac.in
*   **Hyderabad:** training-hyd@cdac.in
*   **Mumbai (Kharghar):** course_kh@cdac.in
*   **Noida:** acts-noida@cdac.in
*   **Pune (ACTS):** actssupport@cdac.in

### **Technical Support for Payments**
If candidates face issues during the online payment process for the first or second installment of course fees, they are advised to contact **C-DAC Mumbai** at **course-enquiry@cdac.in** or the specific phone numbers provided in the Admission Booklet.
