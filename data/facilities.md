## Facilities

The several facilities provided to students, ranging from residential and dining options to placement support and the technical infrastructure required for both online and physical learning.

### **Hostel and Canteen Facilities**
For students enrolled in **physical courses**, hostel and canteen facilities are available at select C-DAC training centres.
*   **Allocation Policy:** Allotments are made on a **first-come, first-served basis**, though **outstation students** are given preference.
*   **Alternative Support:** Centres that do not offer on-site hostels may provide assistance to help students find suitable boarding and lodging nearby.
*   **Specific Availability:** 
    *   Centres with **both hostel and canteen** include C-DAC Bengaluru, C-DAC Mumbai (Kharghar), C-DAC Noida, IACSD Pune, MET-IIT Nashik, and Sunbeam (Karad and Pune).
    *   **C-DAC Hyderabad** offers hostel facilities specifically for **female students** only.
    *   Centres offering **only canteen facilities** include AIT (YCC) Mumbai, C-DAC New Delhi, C-DAC Pune, MET-IIT Mumbai, and USM’s Shriram Mantri VITA Mumbai.
*   **Requests:** Interested candidates must send accommodation requests to the Administrative Officers at their respective centres after admission lists are published.

### **Common Campus Placement Programme (CCPP)**
C-DAC provides extensive placement assistance through its CCPP, which has a history of shaping the careers of over 3 lakh IT professionals.
*   **Regional Hubs:** Placements are organized region-wise at **Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Noida, and Pune**. All training centres are attached to one of these regions to ensure students nationwide have a fair chance.
*   **Corporate Connections:** More than **400 companies**, including major names like Amazon, Cisco, IBM, and TCS, have participated in recruitment.
*   **Placement Orientation:** The facility includes special training in **aptitude, effective communication, and interview skills** to prepare students for the industry.

### **Academic and Technical Infrastructure**
The programmes are designed to be intensive and high-tech, requiring specific infrastructure depending on the mode of delivery.
*   **Learning Environment:** Courses consist of **900 hours** of theory, lab, and project work, plus 300 hours of self-study. In most centres, this involves **6-8 hours of sessions daily**, six days a week.
*   **Faculty Access:** Students learn from expert faculty from both C-DAC and the ICT industry.
*   **Online Course Requirements:** For students in online programmes, C-DAC does not provide hardware; students must have their own **laptop or PC** with a webcam, microphone, and a minimum **25 Mbps high-speed internet connection**. 
*   **Hardware Specifications:** Recommended configurations generally include at least an **Intel Core i3 or i5 processor and 8 GB of RAM**. Specific courses like Artificial Intelligence (PGCP-AI) may require a dedicated **4 GB Graphics Card**.

### **Training Centres**
Facilities are delivered through a network of **C-DAC centres** (such as those in Pune, Mumbai, and New Delhi) and **Authorised Training Centres (ATC)** in cities like Bhubaneswar, Indore, and Jaipur. These centres are equipped to handle specific seat capacities for either online or physical delivery modes.
