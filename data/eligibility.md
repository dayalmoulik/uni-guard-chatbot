## Eligibility Criteria (General)

The eligibility criteria for C-DAC’s Post Graduate Certificate Programmes (formerly PG Diploma courses) for the February 2026 batch consist of both common requirements and course-specific qualifications. 

### **Common Eligibility Criteria**
The baseline requirements applicable to all PG Certificate Programmes include:
*   **Engineering/Technology Graduates:** A degree in Engineering or Technology (10+2+4 or 10+3+3 years) in fields such as IT, Computer Science, Electronics, Telecommunications, Electrical, or Instrumentation.
*   **MSc/MS Graduates:** A Post Graduate degree (10+2+3+2 years) in Computer Science, IT, or Electronics.

### **Course-Specific Qualifications**
In addition to the common criteria, certain programmes allow candidates with other qualifications to apply:
*   **PGCP-AC, PGCP-BDA, and PGCP-MC:** These programmes also accept graduates in **any discipline of Engineering**, MCA, MCM, or those with a Post Graduate degree in **Physics, Mathematics, or Statistics**. Additionally, post-graduates in Management with a graduation in IT, Computer Science, or Computer Applications are eligible.
*   **PGCP-AI (Artificial Intelligence):** Eligibility includes the common criteria, any Engineering discipline, MCA, MCM, or a Post Graduate degree in Physics, Mathematics, or Statistics.
*   **PGCP-ITISS, PGCP-HPCSA, and PGCP-ASSD:** These courses accept candidates with the common criteria or an **MCA**; ITISS and HPCSA also accept Post Graduate degrees in **Mathematics or Statistics**.
*   **PGCP-FBD and PGCP-CSF:** These allow any Engineering graduate, MCA/MCM, or post-graduates in Physics, Mathematics, or Statistics.
*   **PGCP-RAT (Robotics):** Specifically includes graduates in **Mechatronics or Mechanical Engineering**.
*   **PGCP-ESD and PGCP-VLSI:** These programmes strictly follow only the common eligibility criteria.

### **Minimum Percentage Requirements**
*   **General Requirement:** Most courses require a minimum of **55% marks** in the qualifying examination.
*   **Artificial Intelligence:** The PGCP-AI programme requires a higher minimum of **60% marks**.
*   The minimum percentage is calculated based on the norms of the university from which the candidate graduated.

### **Provisional Admission for Final-Year Students**
*   **No age restriction** exists for admission to these programmes.
*   Candidates appearing for their **final degree examination in 2026** may apply for provisional admission.
*   They must complete all parts of their qualifying degree examination by the **date of joining the course**.
*   Proof of passing with the required minimum marks must be submitted to C-DAC by **June 30, 2026**.

### **Important Verification Rules**
*   Candidates must prove they meet the eligibility criteria at the time of admission at their allocated centre.
*   If a candidate is found ineligible at any stage of the process, their **admission will be cancelled** immediately.
*   If a candidate is found ineligible during document verification at registration, the **first installment fee (Rs 10,000 + GST) is non-refundable**.
*   Candidates with degrees not explicitly listed may verify their eligibility by emailing their certificates to **actssupport@cdac.in** before the C-CAT application deadline.
