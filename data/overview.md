## Overview
The **Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC)** is a scientific society established in 1988 under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Government of India. It serves as a premier institution for high-end **Research and Development (R&D)** in IT, electronics, and associated areas, including high-performance computing, cyber security, and software technologies.

In 1993, C-DAC established the **Advanced Computing Training School (ACTS)**. The school was created to meet the rising demand for skilled manpower in the **Information Communication Technologies (ICT) industry** and to supplement C-DAC's own intellectual resource base for cutting-edge research.

### **Key Aspects of C-DAC ACTS**
*   **Educational Offerings:** ACTS designs and delivers various postgraduate and undergraduate degree and certificate programmes. It also provides specialized ICT training for national and state governments, strategic sectors, corporate industries, and international students.
*   **Widespread Training Network:** Programmes are offered through C-DAC’s own centres (located in cities like Bengaluru, Mumbai, Noida, and Pune) as well as through **Authorised Training Centres (ATC)** in cities such as Bhubaneswar, Jaipur, and Indore.
*   **Global Presence:** C-DAC has established state-of-the-art IT Training Centres in many developing countries, including Vietnam, Ghana, Uzbekistan, and Egypt, and has trained students from over **100 countries**.
*   **Proven Impact:** To date, more than **3 lakh students** have graduated from C-DAC. Many of these graduates are now successful entrepreneurs or employees at premier Indian and multi-national ICT companies.
*   **Recognition:** C-DAC was awarded the 'Most Popular Training Institute in India' at the Electronics For You Awards.

### **Current Programme Structure**
C-DAC ACTS currently offers **12 specialized 24-week full-time PG Certificate Programmes** (formerly rebranded from PG Diploma courses). These courses, such as Advanced Computing (PGCP-AC) and Big Data Analytics (PGCP-BDA), are 40-credit programmes involving 1200 hours of study. The curriculum is developed in consultation with domain experts from both academia and the ICT industry to ensure students are prepared for current technology scenarios.
