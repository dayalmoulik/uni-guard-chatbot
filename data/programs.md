## List of PG Certificate Programmes

The **Post Graduate Certificate Programmes (PGCP)**, formerly known as PG Diploma courses, are intensive **24-week, full-time** technical courses designed by C-DAC to prepare students for the requirements of the ICT industry. These programmes are worth **40 credits** and involve a total of **1200 hours** of study, which includes 900 hours dedicated to theory, lab work, and projects, plus 300 hours of self-study.

### **General Programme Highlights**
According to the Admission Booklet, these programmes are developed in consultation with domain experts from C-DAC, academia, and the industry to ensure they meet current technology standards. Key features include:
*   **Daily Schedule:** Most centres conduct sessions for **6 to 8 hours a day**, six days a week.
*   **Expert Instruction:** Courses are delivered by faculty from C-DAC and the ICT industry with widespread domain knowledge.
*   **Integrated Training:** Beyond technical skills, the programmes include special training in **aptitude, effective communication, and interview skills**.
*   **Placement Support:** Students receive extensive placement orientation and access to **Common Campus Placement Programmes (CCPP)** across various regions.
*   **Evaluations:** The curriculum includes continuous internal assessments, as well as **Centralised Mid-Course and Course-End Examinations** conducted physically at identified test centres.

### **Key Course Details and Modules**
The Admission Booklet provide specific insights into the core focus and curriculum of several popular programmes:

*   **PGCP-AC:** This is C-DAC's most popular course, aiming to groom engineering graduates for advanced computing roles. Core modules include **C++ and Java programming, Algorithms, Database Technologies, Web Programming, and Microsoft .Net Technologies**.
*   **PGCP-BDA:** Targets those seeking careers as **Data Scientists or Business Analysts**. It covers Python/R programming, **Hadoop and Map Reduce technologies**, Machine Learning, and Data Visualization.
*   **PGCP-AI:** Focuses on providing in-depth knowledge of **Natural Language Processing, Deep Learning**, and Machine Learning using computational tools and case studies.
*   **PGCP-ESD:** Designed for engineers to handle both hardware and software design. It emphasizes **Microcontroller Interfacing, Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS)**, and the Internet of Things (IoT).
*   **PGCP-VLSI:** A pioneering course for the VLSI design industry, covering **Verilog HDL, System Verilog**, Verification using UVM, and ASIC design.
*   **PGCP-CSF:** Developed for practitioners in defensive cyber security and ethical hacking, covering **Network Essentials, Penetration Testing**, and Cyber Forensics.
*   **PGCP-RAT:** An inter-disciplinary course covering **Mechanical and Electronics design**, Robot Operating System (ROS), and AI/ML-based robotic vision.
